Education of Shaheed Bhagat Singh

Bhagat Singh was not only a brave freedom fighter but also a curious reader and thoughtful student. His education helped develop his knowledge of history, politics and revolutionary ideas.

1. Primary Education – Banga

Bhagat Singh began his early education at a primary school in his native village of Banga, in the Lyallpur district of Punjab, which is now in Pakistan. He received his foundational education there before moving to Lahore for further studies.

2. Dayanand Anglo-Vedic (DAV) School, Lahore

He later studied at DAV High School in Lahore. The school was associated with the Arya Samaj educational tradition. During these years, he developed a growing interest in India's history, national issues and the freedom movement.

3. National College, Lahore

Bhagat Singh later joined National College in Lahore, an institution established by Lala Lajpat Rai and others to encourage nationalist education. The college played an important role in shaping his political awareness.

4. Subjects and Intellectual Interests

Bhagat Singh developed a strong interest in history, politics, philosophy and revolutionary movements around the world. He read books and writings about freedom, equality, socialism and political change.

5. Education Beyond the Classroom

His learning was not limited to school and college. He read widely, discussed political ideas and learned about international revolutionary movements. These experiences helped him form his views on independence, justice and social equality.

6. How Education Shaped Bhagat Singh

His education and reading encouraged him to think critically about British rule and India's future. He became known not only for his courage but also for his writing, ideas and commitment to social change.

Legacy: Bhagat Singh's life reminds us that education is not just about passing examinations; it can also help people understand society and work towards a better future.
