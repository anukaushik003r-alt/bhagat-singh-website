The Life Journey of Shaheed Bhagat Singh

About Bhagat Singh

Bhagat Singh was born on 28 September 1907 in Banga, Punjab (now in Pakistan). He belonged to a patriotic Sikh family that actively participated in India's freedom movement.

From childhood he loved reading books and learning about great leaders. The Jallianwala Bagh massacre deeply affected him and strengthened his desire to fight for India's independence.

He joined the Hindustan Socialist Republican Association (HSRA) and became one of India's most fearless revolutionaries.

On 23 March 1931, Bhagat Singh, Rajguru and Sukhdev were martyred in Lahore Jail. His sacrifice made him immortal in the hearts of Indians.
