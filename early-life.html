Early Life

Childhood and Education of Shaheed Bhagat Singh

Childhood

Bhagat Singh was born on 28 September 1907 in Banga village of Punjab. He belonged to a patriotic family where many members were involved in India's freedom movement.

Family Influence

His father Kishan Singh and his uncles inspired him towards patriotism. From a young age, Bhagat Singh developed a strong desire to free India from British rule.

Education

He studied at National College, Lahore. He was a brilliant student and had great interest in history, politics and revolutionary ideas.

Jallianwala Bagh Incident

The Jallianwala Bagh massacre of 1919 deeply affected him. This incident strengthened his determination to fight for India's independence.
