Bhagat Singh's Life Journey

A timeline of key events in his life

September 28, 1907

Birth

Bhagat Singh was born in Banga village, Punjab (now in Pakistan).

1919

Jallianwala Bagh Massacre

This event had a deep impact on the young Bhagat Singh.

1926

Naujawan Bharat Sabha

He helped establish the Naujawan Bharat Sabha to organize young people.

1928

Death of Lala Lajpat Rai

Lala Lajpat Rai died after being injured during a lathi charge at a protest against the Simon Commission.

April 8, 1929

Central Legislative Assembly Protest

Bhagat Singh and Batukeshwar Dutt threw bombs in the Assembly to protest and were arrested.

March 23, 1931

Martyrdom

Bhagat Singh, Rajguru, and Sukhdev were executed at Lahore Central Jail.
