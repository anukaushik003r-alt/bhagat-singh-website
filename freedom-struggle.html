Freedom Struggle

Bhagat Singh's Revolutionary Journey

Beginning of Revolution

Bhagat Singh dedicated his life to India's independence struggle. He believed that courage and sacrifice were necessary to achieve freedom.

Naujawan Bharat Sabha

In 1926, Bhagat Singh founded Naujawan Bharat Sabha to inspire young Indians and spread awareness about the freedom movement.

Hindustan Socialist Republican Association

Bhagat Singh became an important member of HSRA. The organization worked against British rule and fought for India's independence.

Assembly Bomb Case

On 8 April 1929, Bhagat Singh and Batukeshwar Dutt threw non-lethal bombs in the Central Legislative Assembly to protest against unfair laws.

Jail Life

During his imprisonment, Bhagat Singh went on a hunger strike demanding equal rights for Indian political prisoners.

Martyrdom

Bhagat Singh, Rajguru and Sukhdev were executed on 23 March 1931. Their sacrifice inspired millions of Indians.
